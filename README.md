# Colonist-Pride_Rimworld  
A Rimworld mod to give colonists pride in their work.  

## Details  
* Pride: Colonists will be given a mood boost when they create high quality items  
* Shame: Colonists will receive a mood negative when they create items of low quality  
* Heartbreak: Colonists will receive a mood negative when a high quality item that they created gets destroyed  
* Relief: Colonists will be given a small mood boost when low quality items they created gets destroyed
* Other colonists will think more highly of colonists who churn out high quality items  
* Fully adjustable in the settings
